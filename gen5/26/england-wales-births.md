::: notes
This is page has links to a large data file in Excel format that breaks down births in England and Wales by a variety of factors such as age of the father and mother, singleton versus multiple births, day of the year, and many others. The data looks to be ideal for classroom demonstrations and could also be useful for student projects. Students should be advised to look at small subsets of these datasets so as not to overwhelm themselves.

The data is available under [an open government license][ref-ons-2026] which allows free use as long as you acknowledge the source of information.

Office for National Statistics. Births in England and Wales: birth registrations. Available in [html format][ref-ons-nodate].

[ref-ons-nodate]: https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/birthsdeathsandmarriages/livebirths/datasets/birthsinenglandandwalesbirthregistrations

:::
