::: notes
The International Statistical Institute shared a press release discussing the case of Andreas Georgiou, an economist/statistician and the former president of a major Greek statistical organization. Dr. Georgiou had released some data on the Greek economy that had upset Greek politicians. The numbers were fine, but they reflected a weak economy. Dr. Georgiou was charged with harming the national interest and sentenced to two years of probation.

This led to a large number of protests from statistical organizations like the International Statistical Institute. Recently the Greek Supreme Court reversed the conviction and exonerated Dr. Georgiou. This press release provides details about that exoneration.
