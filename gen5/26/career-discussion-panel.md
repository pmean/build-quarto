I am part of a panel discussion ("What Consulting Statisticians Wish They Had Known as Students or Early-Career Professionals") and the organizers asked for a three to five sentence introduction. Here is what I gave them.

<!---more--->

Steve Simon graduated with a PhD in Statistics from the University of Iowa in 1982 and has worked at two Universities, a federal agency, and a hospital. He also has a part-time practice in statistical consulting. He is quite proud of a website that he started in 1999 that has grown to over two thousand pages, covering topics in statistics, evidence-based medicine, and research ethics.

Here are some things I might want to cover in my part of the panel discussion.

-   The electives that you take outside your program are just as important as your required courses.
-   Embrace continuing education and stay current with new computing advances.
-   No one likes a wishy-washy statistician. Be decisive.
